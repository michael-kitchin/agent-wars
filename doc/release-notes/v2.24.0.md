A turn-based grand strategy game on a real-Earth hex map. Your opponents are large language models you choose through OpenRouter. Coding agents wrote the code; the story is told in the Agent Wars series in [*Standing Orders*](https://standingorders.substack.com).

## Downloads

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `agent-wars-2.24.0-windows-x64.zip` | Windows, x64 |
| `agent-wars-2.24.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS on Apple silicon |
| `agent-wars-2.24.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | Linux, x64 |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | SHA-256 checksums for the three archives |

These are unsigned side-load builds, so Windows and macOS warn before the first launch.

- **Windows:** unzip, then run `Agent Wars v2.24.0.exe`. If SmartScreen stops it, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** unzip, then open `Agent Wars v2.24.0.app`. If macOS blocks it, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.
- **Linux:** extract the archive, then run `./agent-wars`. That launcher handles Chromium's sandbox when the archive can't keep `chrome-sandbox` owned by root. On Ubuntu 23.10 and later, where AppArmor blocks unprivileged user namespaces, it starts without the sandbox and says so.

To check a download, compare its SHA-256 with the line in `SHA256SUMS.txt`: `Get-FileHash <file>` in PowerShell, `shasum -a 256 <file>` on macOS, or `sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS.txt` on Linux.

## Before you play

- **Bring your own OpenRouter key.** Start a match. The console on the right starts collapsed; click **Command** and open a commander's card. Paste that opponent's key, choose a model, and click **Start**. Each opponent has its own key. A new game fills only the first opponent from the key you used last. OpenRouter bills your account for the commanders' model calls. Without a key and a model on at least one commander the app still runs, **Start** stays off, and that opponent makes no model calls. The key stays on your computer and is sent only to OpenRouter ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/michael-kitchin/agent-wars/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).
- **The map is in the download.** Shaded relief is packed with the app, so the map does not use a tile server. The first time a regional map is shown, its tiles unpack on this computer. A commander's model calls still need an internet connection.
- **Non-commercial use.** The app ships earth data under non-commercial terms, so the application as distributed is for non-commercial use. See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](https://github.com/michael-kitchin/agent-wars/blob/main/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
- **Saves.** A save from 2.12.0 through 2.23.0 still opens. A save from 2.10.0 or earlier will not load in 2.24.0.

How a match plays is in [doc/how-to-play.md](https://github.com/michael-kitchin/agent-wars/blob/main/doc/how-to-play.md).

## What's in 2.24.0

- **Water names.** The map now names oceans, seas, gulfs, bays, straits, channels, and large lakes. The names come from Natural Earth's 1:10m marine polygons and lakes (`ne_10m_geography_marine_polys`, `ne_10m_lakes`). Sounds, fjords, lagoons, inlets, reefs, and rivers are not named (`MARINE_LABEL_CLASSES`). A sea, gulf, bay, strait, or channel with a Natural Earth `scalerank` of 2 or less is major water (`MAJOR_MARINE_MAX_SCALERANK`). A lake is major at 1 or less (`MAJOR_LAKE_MAX_SCALERANK`), and a lake ranked above 6 is left off (`LAKE_LABEL_MAX_SCALERANK`). Everything else that is kept is minor water.

- **Which water names show at which zoom.** Water names follow the same tiers as land names (`visibleWaterLabelKinds`). Below zoom 4 (`COUNTRY_LABEL_MIN_ZOOM`) the map shows ocean names, whatever their stored minimum zoom, as it does for continent names. From zoom 4 it adds major water. A battle, and the close view that draws the smaller hexes, drops the ocean names and shows major and minor water. A major sea, gulf, bay, or lake appears one level after its Natural Earth minimum zoom (`WATER_LABEL_ZOOM_DELAY`), and only once its largest anchor covers at least 40 CSS pixels on screen (`WATER_LABEL_MIN_RADIUS_PX`). A minor one may appear up to 1.5 levels before its minimum (`LABEL_MIN_ZOOM_SLACK`), with the same 40 px test. A strait or channel is too thin for that test, so it waits two levels past its minimum instead (`NARROW_WATER_ZOOM_DELAY`).

- **How water names look.** Water names are italic, at weight 400, in the theme's own face: Agent Wars Map Sans on Standard and Night, Agent Wars Map Serif on Topographic and Nautical. Ocean names are 12 px capitals with 0.3 em letter-spacing. Major water is 9.75 px and minor water is 8.25 px, both in the name's own case (`MAP_LABEL_KIND_STYLES`). They keep those sizes in every view and are painted at full strength (`WATER_LABEL_OPACITY`). Each theme has its own water ink, a quieter blue than its land names, with a halo in its water color at 0.8 alpha (`waterLabelColors` in `src/shared/mapThemes.ts`). The ink is `#2a5676` on Standard, `#1f5a80` on Topographic, `#3d6a8a` on Nautical, and `#8fb0c9` on Night. The app now ships the 400-weight italic Noto Sans and Noto Serif files for these names.

- **Land names come first.** Land names are placed first and do not move for a water name. A water name tries its anchor, then the spot it used last frame, then the nearest clear spot up to 36 px away in 12 px steps (`WATER_LABEL_NUDGE_MAX_PX`). It must clear every land name and every water name already drawn, and its center must stay at least 24 px inside the view edge (`LABEL_VIEW_INSET_PX`). With no clear spot that close, the name is left off. One frame draws at most 40 water names (`MAX_WATER_LABELS_PER_FRAME`), and they are painted under the land names (`settleWaterLabels` in `src/shared/mapWaterLabelPlacement.ts`).

## Known limits

- There is no in-app list of saves, and no turn replay.
- Settings, Help, How to play, the Build and Forces tabs, and Replay on a turn report are placeholders in this version.
- On macOS, Quit closes the window and the app stays in the dock.
- On the world map, outside a battle, a route or a played-back move that crosses the 180° meridian runs off the edge of the map. The destination hex is drawn on the opposite edge.
- The macOS build is for Apple silicon only, and it hasn't been opened on a Mac yet, so its first-launch steps are untested.
- Model quality varies. A model without tool calls plays without the planning tools.
- This is mostly a personal R&D project. Issues and pull requests are welcome; responses and acceptance are best-effort. Security reports go through the repository's **Security** tab.
