# Contractions

House-style overlay, not an authorship verdict. Always listed. Default fix rewrites
unless `--severity definitive`. The apostrophe is ASCII `'`. Prefer a `* not` /
`cannot` pair over an overlapping copula or modal (`are not` before `they are`,
`is not` before `it is`). Among those, longest expanded form first (`will not`
before `I will`). When a guard is unsure, leave the span expanded.

Grep `missing-contraction` in `references/mechanicalSigns.md`, then apply the Guards
in SKILL.md and the `guard` column below. This table is the only pair list. Do not
invent `there're`, `ain't`, `I'd've`, `shan't`, `what'd` from `what would`, or other
forms. `what had` still maps to `what'd` under `aux-had`. Locative `here is` and
object-pronoun copulas stay expanded; see SKILL.md Contractions.

Format of the fence: `expanded | contracted | guard`. Guard is `none`, `aux-have`,
`aux-had`, `hortative`, or `not-refrain`.
